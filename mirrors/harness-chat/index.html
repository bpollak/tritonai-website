Use TritonAI Harness from your iPhone.

TritonAI Harness Chat connects your iPhone to TritonAI Harness on your Mac or PC. You send a request from the phone. The Harness carries it out on your computer, using the calendar, mail, files, plugins and skills you have set up there, and the result appears in the app. After each answer it suggests follow-ups you can send with one tap.

Works with TritonAI Harness

The app sends requests to TritonAI Harness on your computer.

TritonAI Harness runs on your Mac or PC with your signed-in accounts, plugins and skills. The app is a second way to use it. A request typed on the phone is sent to your computer, the Harness carries it out there, and the answer is sent back to the phone.

The same plugins and skills

The app uses the plugins and skills already installed in the Harness on your computer. Nothing is configured separately on the phone.

A single conversation

All requests and answers appear in one chat. The app does not ask you to choose a project, thread or model.

Shared between phone and computer

Requests sent from the phone also appear in the Harness on your computer. Files the Harness saves on the computer can be opened on the phone.

Examples

Requests that use your calendar, mail and files.

Each example shows a request, the sources the Harness reads to answer it, and the result returned to the phone. Select an example to play it.

Status

The app shows each step as it happens.

A status line at the top of the chat names the current step. Tap it during a request to see the full list of steps. After the request finishes, "See what it did" under the answer shows the same list.

  • Ready when you are when no request is running
  • Searching your files, Reading your calendar and similar during a request
  • I need your OK to keep going when a step requires your approval
  • Done when the answer is in the chat

Control

The assistant asks for approval before it acts.

Requests that only read information run without interruption. Steps that send, change or open something wait for you.

  • Email and messages are drafts. The assistant writes them into the chat for you to review and send yourself.
  • Actions require approval. Sending a message, changing a calendar event and opening a web page each show an approval card. A setting extends this to every step.
  • Requests can be stopped. The stop button ends the current request immediately.
  • Each answer has a record. The steps taken are listed under every answer.

The app

The app has four tabs.

Follow-ups you can tap

After an answer, the assistant offers the likely next steps as buttons, such as drafting an agenda for a meeting it just found. One tap sends it.

Share from other apps

In Safari, Files or Photos, tap Share and choose Harness to send a page, document or picture to the assistant with your request.

Ready-made routines

The Scheduled tab includes routines such as a morning brief and an end of day wrap-up. Turn one on and it runs on weekdays.

Drafts you can use

A drafted email appears as a card. Copy it, or open it in your mail app to review and send yourself.

Setup

Setup takes about two minutes.

  1. 1

    Open TritonAI Harness

    On your Mac or PC, open TritonAI Harness and choose to connect a phone. It shows a code.

  2. 2

    Scan the code

    Point your iPhone at the screen. There is no account to create and nothing to sign in to.

  3. 3

    Send a request

    Keep TritonAI Harness open on your computer. On the same Wi-Fi the app connects directly. With Tailscale installed on both devices it also connects from other networks.

First get the app from the App Store. The setup guide has each step in detail. The app also includes a demo mode that runs without a computer.

Privacy

Your requests go only to your own computer.

Text you type or dictate and files you attach are sent from the phone to TritonAI Harness on your computer, and to no other destination. If you allow notifications, the app also sends a push token to a relay so a notification can reach your phone. A notification never includes your request or the answer. The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

How does it relate to TritonAI Harness?

The app is a phone client for TritonAI Harness. The Harness runs on your Mac or PC and carries out requests with your accounts, plugins and skills. The app lets you send requests, approve steps and read results when you are away from that computer.

Which version of TritonAI Harness do I need?

Version 0.4.2 or later, which is the current stable release. Earlier versions cannot connect to the app. To check, open TritonAI Harness on your computer and look at the version in its settings.

Is the app finished?

No. It is in early beta testing. It is being tried by a small group of UC San Diego staff, and parts of it will change. If something does not work, email tritonai@ucsd.edu.

Who is it for?

UC San Diego staff who use TritonAI Harness on a Mac or PC. The app is a companion to the Harness and needs it to do real work.

Does my computer have to be on?

Yes. Requests are carried out on your computer, so it must be awake with TritonAI Harness open. If the computer cannot be reached, the app keeps your message and sends it when the connection returns.

Do I have to be on the same Wi-Fi?

By default, yes. With Tailscale installed on both the phone and the computer, the app also connects from other networks.

Can it send email or change my calendar on its own?

No. It writes drafts and shows an approval card before sending or changing anything. Which accounts it can use depends on the plugins turned on in TritonAI Harness on your computer.

Can I try it without a computer?

Yes. Choose "Try a demo first" on the opening screen. The demo runs on the phone and does not use any of your information.

Where do I get the app?

It is a free download from the App Store. It requires iOS 18 or later.

What does it cost?

Nothing. The app is free.

Where do I get help?

The setup and support page has step-by-step instructions, or email tritonai@ucsd.edu.
