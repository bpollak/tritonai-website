Choose by need

Start with the task and the data it touches

UC San Diego supports several AI services. Which one you should reach for depends on what you are doing, what data is involved, where the work already happens, and how closely the result needs to be reviewed.

Available services

Supported AI services

T

TritonGPT

The campus AI platform. Pick a model, work with your own documents, use assistants that know about campus.

Explore TritonGPT →
Z

Zoom AI Companion

Meeting transcription, summaries, questions, and other enabled Zoom experiences.

Review Zoom guidance →
C

Copilot with Data Protection

Protected chat for drafting, summarizing, generating images, and looking things up.

Open Microsoft 365 →
M

Copilot for Microsoft 365

AI built into Word, Excel, Outlook, and the rest of the supported Microsoft 365 apps.

Review access details →
G

Gemini and Gemini Notebook

Google's tools. Gemini for general work, Gemini Notebook for research and notes tied to sources you supply.

Review Google guidance →
