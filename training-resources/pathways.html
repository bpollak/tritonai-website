AI Learning Pathways

  1. TritonAI
  2. Learn
  3. AI Learning Pathways

Pick the pathway that matches your job. Each one starts with the rules that apply to you and ends with something specific to go do.

Choose your route

A next step for every role

Start with what is expected of you, then work up from orientation to actually using the tools well.

Student pathway

Students

Learn with confidence without losing your own voice.
  1. Review course and instructor expectations.
  2. Learn prompting and verification.
  3. Use TritonGPT for study support without misrepresenting authorship.
Start with prompting →

Teaching pathway

Faculty and instructors

Set clear expectations and design learning with evidence.
  1. Set transparent course expectations.
  2. Explore instructional patterns and limitations.
  3. Evaluate learning outcomes and accessibility.
Explore instructional AI →

Workplace pathway

Staff

Point an approved tool at the work that keeps coming back.
  1. Complete AI Foundations.
  2. Practice with approved tools and non-sensitive examples.
  3. Bring a recurring workflow to a use-case conversation.
Browse training →

Builder pathway

Developers

Get from picking a model to running a tested service.
  1. Understand model and data choices.
  2. Build a narrow prototype with evaluation.
  3. Plan ownership, hosting, and operations.
Get started building →

Leadership pathway

Campus leaders

Back work that can be measured and has an owner.
  1. Learn the portfolio and trust model.
  2. Identify measurable, owned problems.
  3. Sponsor staged pilots with human accountability.
Read the strategy →

Built for access

Accessible formats

Recordings need captions, a transcript, a descriptive title, and a poster image or text alternative that says something. Demonstration videos need visible controls, and everything they show has to be available to someone who cannot watch the motion.
