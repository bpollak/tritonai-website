    How to use this series

    The TritonAI Learning Journey

    A guided path that moves from understanding AI, to using the campus tools, to building with them. Start with video 1 and progress in order.

    • Learn at your own pace: The full series takes about 3 hours.
    • Learn together: This series works well as a team activity too. Watch one video per meeting and use the discussion points beneath each one to spark a group discussion.
    • Pick up where you left off: Your progress is saved automatically in your browser, so you can leave and come back anytime.

    Go further

    Digital Education Council: Generative AI Course for Higher Education Institutions

    For a deeper dive into AI across higher education, UC San Diego offers access to the Digital Education Council's Generative AI Course for Higher Education Institutions. The course is organized into three streams, one each for staff, faculty, and students, so everyone on campus has a path suited to their role.

    You can work through it independently, or set up a learning community with your team or a cross-functional group and meet regularly to discuss the videos together. Communities can appoint one lead for the whole course, or rotate the lead so a different person heads up the discussion for each section of chapters. Most learning communities take about three months to complete the course.
