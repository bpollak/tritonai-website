Inaugural faculty event

Faculty AI Symposium

UC San Diego faculty came together to explore how artificial intelligence is reshaping teaching, research, and academic practice.

Convened by Chancellor Pradeep K. Khosla and the San Diego Division of the Academic Senate, the symposium featured faculty-led presentations and expert panel discussions. The event centered a community-driven approach to AI at UC San Diego.

Panel discussions

Facilitating and Validating Learning in the Age of AI

Moderated by Tricia Bertram-Gallant, Director of the Academic Integrity Office and the Triton Testing Center. Panelists examined how AI is changing the conditions for learning and what it means to design, facilitate, and assess student work when AI tools are widely available.

Watch the panel →

Responsible AI Adoption and Development

Moderated by Virginia de Sa, Professor of Cognitive Science and Interim Co-Director of the Halıcıoğlu Data Science Institute. Panelists explored how AI is being used across teaching and research, along with its implications for academic inquiry, collaboration, and the role of faculty.

Faculty use-case presentations

Across twelve talks, faculty showed how AI is being tested in teaching, advising, and personal decision support. The presentations cover personalized learning and tutoring, question-bank and lecture development, programming and writing instruction, and undergraduate AI literacy.

Other talks examine classroom chatbots and productive struggle, along with K–12 student curiosity. Health decision support and an AI faculty advisor extend the discussion beyond the classroom.
