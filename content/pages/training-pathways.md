<div class="learning-section-heading"><p class="home-kicker">Choose your route</p><h2 id="choose-pathway-heading">A next step for every role</h2><p>Same foundation, five routes — each shaped around what you actually do.</p></div>
<div class="learning-pathway-grid">
<article class="learning-pathway-card"><header><span class="learning-pathway-icon glyphicon glyphicon-education" aria-hidden="true"></span><div><p>Student pathway</p><h3>Students</h3><span>Learn with confidence without losing your own voice.</span></div></header><ol class="learning-pathway-steps"><li>Check how each of your courses handles AI — instructor guidance comes first, and every class is different.</li><li>Learn prompting and verification, and use TritonGPT for study support without misrepresenting what's yours.</li></ol><a class="learning-pathway-action" href="/training-resources/prompting/index.html">Start with prompting <span aria-hidden="true">→</span></a></article>
<article class="learning-pathway-card"><header><span class="learning-pathway-icon glyphicon glyphicon-pencil" aria-hidden="true"></span><div><p>Teaching pathway</p><h3>Faculty and instructors</h3><span>Set clear terms and design learning with evidence.</span></div></header><ol class="learning-pathway-steps"><li>Watch the <a href="/training-resources/videos/index.html">TritonAI Discovery Series</a> — get oriented on what's available and approved.</li><li>Make your course policy explicit: what's allowed, what isn't, and why.</li><li>Try instructional patterns, then check what students actually learned — outcomes and accessibility included.</li></ol><a class="learning-pathway-action" href="/training-resources/faculty-ai-symposium.html">Explore ideas from the Faculty AI Symposium <span aria-hidden="true">→</span></a></article>
<article class="learning-pathway-card"><header><span class="learning-pathway-icon glyphicon glyphicon-briefcase" aria-hidden="true"></span><div><p>Workplace pathway</p><h3>Staff</h3><span>Put an approved tool to work on the tasks that keep coming back.</span></div></header><ol class="learning-pathway-steps"><li>Watch the <a href="/training-resources/videos/index.html">TritonAI Discovery Series</a> — what's approved, what's available.</li><li>Pick one repeating task — a routine email, a long document, messy notes — and practice on non-sensitive examples.</li><li>Double-check the AI's output, then share your use case at a Community of Practice to compare approaches with colleagues.</li></ol><a class="learning-pathway-action" href="/training-resources/index.html">Browse training <span aria-hidden="true">→</span></a></article>
<article class="learning-pathway-card"><header><span class="learning-pathway-icon glyphicon glyphicon-console" aria-hidden="true"></span><div><p>Builder pathway</p><h3>Builders and developers</h3><span>Move from using AI to building with it.</span></div></header><ol class="learning-pathway-steps"><li>Watch the <a href="/training-resources/videos/index.html">Discovery Series</a> — the lay of the land before you build.</li><li>Request access to the TritonAI Harness and follow the citizen developer path.</li><li>Build a narrow prototype with evaluation: one workflow, tested, with a human checking the results.</li><li>Plan ownership, hosting, and operations before it's real.</li></ol><a class="learning-pathway-action" href="/developer-apis/start.html">Get started building <span aria-hidden="true">→</span></a></article>
<article class="learning-pathway-card learning-pathway-wide"><header><span class="learning-pathway-icon glyphicon glyphicon-flag" aria-hidden="true"></span><div><p>Leadership pathway</p><h3>Campus leaders</h3><span>Invest in work that can be measured and has an owner.</span></div></header><ol class="learning-pathway-steps"><li>Watch the <a href="/training-resources/videos/index.html">Discovery Series</a> to understand what's available to your teams.</li><li>Learn the portfolio and trust model — what's approved, supported, and why.</li><li>Identify measurable, owned problems, and sponsor staged pilots with human accountability.</li><li>Go further: the <a href="https://connect.digitaleducationcouncil.com/saml/374f4b57-dbb2-4022-a197-24bc04903174/login/7dcc46e4-23d8-4e5f-871f-92650a7252d5">Digital Education Council leaders stream</a>.</li></ol><a class="learning-pathway-action" href="/about/strategy.html">Read the strategy <span aria-hidden="true">→</span></a></article>
