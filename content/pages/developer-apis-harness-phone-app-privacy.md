<p class="home-kicker">Privacy statement</p>
<h2 id="phone-privacy-intro-heading">Privacy in TritonAI Harness Chat</h2>
<p class="hub-lede">TritonAI Harness Chat connects your iPhone to TritonAI Harness running on your own Mac or PC. What you type, dictate, or attach goes from your phone to that computer, and the answers come back the same way.</p>
<p>This statement covers the iPhone app. The <a href="/developer-apis/harness-privacy.html">TritonAI Harness Privacy Statement</a> describes what your computer does with a request.</p>
</div>
<div class="col-md-4">
<aside class="hub-note-card">
<strong>No data collected by the app</strong>
<span>The app has no analytics, advertising, or tracking, and it sends nothing to its developer.</span>
</aside>
</div>
</div>
</section>

<section class="hub-section hub-section-sand hub-full-bleed" aria-labelledby="phone-privacy-data-heading">
<div class="container">
<div class="hub-heading">
<p class="home-kicker">Your information</p>
<h2 id="phone-privacy-data-heading">Where your requests go</h2>
</div>
<div class="row hub-action-grid">
<div class="col-md-4">
<article class="panel panel-default hub-action-card">
<span class="glyphicon glyphicon-transfer" aria-hidden="true"></span>
<h3>Between your phone and your computer</h3>
<p>Requests, attached photos, and answers travel between your phone and the computer you connected, over the network the two share. The app does not send them anywhere else.</p>
</article>
</div>
<div class="col-md-4">
<article class="panel panel-default hub-action-card">
<span class="glyphicon glyphicon-phone" aria-hidden="true"></span>
<h3>Stored on your phone</h3>
<p>The app keeps a copy of your conversation, any messages waiting to send, and a connection key for your computer. The key is held in the iPhone keychain.</p>
</article>
</div>
<div class="col-md-4">
<article class="panel panel-default hub-action-card">
<span class="glyphicon glyphicon-trash" aria-hidden="true"></span>
<h3>Removing it</h3>
<p>Choosing Disconnect in the app's Settings, or deleting the app, removes the conversation and the connection key from your phone. Records on your computer are managed in TritonAI Harness.</p>
</article>
</div>
</div>
</div>
</section>

<section class="hub-section" aria-labelledby="phone-privacy-permissions-heading">
<div class="hub-heading">
<p class="home-kicker">Permissions</p>
<h2 id="phone-privacy-permissions-heading">What the app asks to use</h2>
<p>Each permission is requested the first time you use the feature that needs it.</p>
</div>
<div class="row hub-action-grid">
<div class="col-md-6">
<article class="panel panel-default hub-action-card">
<span class="glyphicon glyphicon-camera" aria-hidden="true"></span>
<h3>Camera and photos</h3>
<p>The camera scans the pairing code on your computer. The camera and photo library are also used when you choose to attach a photo to a request.</p>
</article>
</div>
<div class="col-md-6">
<article class="panel panel-default hub-action-card">
<span class="glyphicon glyphicon-volume-up" aria-hidden="true"></span>
<h3>Microphone and speech recognition</h3>
<p>These are used for dictation when you tap the microphone. Where your iPhone supports it, speech is turned into text on the device.</p>
</article>
</div>
<div class="col-md-6">
<article class="panel panel-default hub-action-card">
<span class="glyphicon glyphicon-signal" aria-hidden="true"></span>
<h3>Local network</h3>
<p>Local network access lets the app reach TritonAI Harness on your computer.</p>
</article>
</div>
<div class="col-md-6">
<article class="panel panel-default hub-action-card">
<span class="glyphicon glyphicon-play-circle" aria-hidden="true"></span>
<h3>Demo</h3>
<p>The built-in demo uses sample answers stored in the app. It does not connect to a computer or use your calendar, mail, or files.</p>
</article>
</div>
</div>
