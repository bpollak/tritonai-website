<p class="home-kicker">Updated November 21, 2026</p>
<h2 id="chatgpt-edu-intro-heading">A systemwide agreement with OpenAI</h2>
<p class="hub-lede">On November 19, the University of California announced a systemwide agreement with OpenAI for ChatGPT Edu. As part of the UC system, UC San Diego will make ChatGPT Edu available under that agreement, though it is not available on campus yet.</p>
<p class="hub-body">Each UC campus is responsible for its own rollout, and UC San Diego is planning its implementation now. We are not publishing launch dates yet and will share timing once we can stand behind it.</p>
<p><a class="btn btn-default" href="https://www.universityofcalifornia.edu/press-room/uc-expands-collaboration-openai-offering-free-access-students-faculty-and-staff-secure">Read the UC Office of the President announcement</a></p>
</div>
</section>

<section class="hub-section hub-section-sand hub-full-bleed" aria-labelledby="chatgpt-edu-announcement-heading">
<div class="container">
<div class="hub-heading">
<p class="home-kicker">Agreement terms</p>
<h2 id="chatgpt-edu-announcement-heading">What the UC agreement covers</h2>
<p>The expanded systemwide agreement covers OpenAI tools including ChatGPT and Codex. Under the terms UC negotiated, data entered into university workspace accounts carries contractual protections.</p>
</div>
<div class="row hub-action-grid">
<div class="col-md-4">
<article class="panel panel-default hub-action-card">
<span class="glyphicon glyphicon-lock" aria-hidden="true"></span>
<h3>No model training</h3>
<p>OpenAI does not use your prompts or the responses you receive to train or improve its models.</p>
</article>
</div>
<div class="col-md-4">
<article class="panel panel-default hub-action-card">
<span class="glyphicon glyphicon-briefcase" aria-hidden="true"></span>
<h3>University content ownership</h3>
<p>The university retains ownership of the content entered into and generated within university workspace accounts.</p>
</article>
</div>
<div class="col-md-4">
<article class="panel panel-default hub-action-card">
<span class="glyphicon glyphicon-check" aria-hidden="true"></span>
<h3>UC security terms apply</h3>
<p>UC security terms take priority over OpenAI's standard terms and include a requirement that OpenAI report any breach to UC.</p>
</article>
</div>
</div>
<div class="row hub-principle-grid">
<div class="col-md-4">
<article>
<span class="glyphicon glyphicon-user" aria-hidden="true"></span>
<div>
<h3>University workspaces only</h3>
<p>These protections apply only to university workspace accounts and do not cover personal ChatGPT accounts. They also do not permit every type of university data; guidance on appropriate data will accompany the rollout.</p>
</div>
</article>
</div>
<div class="col-md-4">
<article>
<span class="glyphicon glyphicon-education" aria-hidden="true"></span>
<div>
<h3>Voluntary use and faculty choice</h3>
<p>UC does not require these tools. Faculty decide whether and how AI is used in their courses.</p>
</div>
</article>
</div>
<div class="col-md-4">
<article>
<span class="glyphicon glyphicon-calendar" aria-hidden="true"></span>
<div>
<h3>Campus rollout scope</h3>
<p>The announcement does not set campus launch dates or mean every named tool, including Codex, will be enabled at UC San Diego at the same time or in the same way. Our campus operating model will determine which tools are offered, to whom, and in what order.</p>
</div>
</article>
</div>
</div>
</div>
</section>

<section class="hub-section hub-section-dark hub-full-bleed" aria-labelledby="chatgpt-edu-why-heading">
<div class="container">
<div class="hub-heading">
<p class="home-kicker">Campus planning</p>
<h2 id="chatgpt-edu-why-heading">Why rollout takes planning</h2>
<p>A systemwide agreement sets the university's contract terms with OpenAI. Each campus must design the local service model before turning accounts on.</p>
</div>
<div class="row hub-number-grid">
<div class="col-md-4">
<article>
<span>01</span>
<h3>Local operating model</h3>
<p>UC San Diego is defining how the service will run on campus: who receives access to which capabilities, how people request them, and how the service will be supported over time.</p>
</article>
</div>
<div class="col-md-4">
<article>
<span>02</span>
<h3>One-year contract horizon</h3>
<p>The current agreement covers one year with a limited allocation. Our model must work at UC San Diego's scale, distribute access fairly across students, faculty, and staff, and remain workable after that year ends.</p>
</article>
</div>
<div class="col-md-4">
<article>
<span>03</span>
<h3>Academic Senate consultation</h3>
<p>The operating model is still in development. The Academic Senate will be consulted on the plan, and it will be shared through campus channels before it takes effect.</p>
</article>
</div>
</div>
</div>
</section>

<section class="hub-section" aria-labelledby="chatgpt-edu-credits-heading">
<div class="hub-heading">
<p class="home-kicker">Service tiers</p>
<h2 id="chatgpt-edu-credits-heading">What an account includes and what uses credits</h2>
<p>As OpenAI describes ChatGPT Edu, access is divided into everyday use included with an account and advanced features that draw on shared credits.</p>
</div>
<div class="row hub-action-grid">
<div class="col-md-6">
<article class="panel panel-default hub-action-card">
<span class="glyphicon glyphicon-comment" aria-hidden="true"></span>
<h3>Everyday use included with an account</h3>
<p>OpenAI's fast, general-purpose models for chat, writing, search, and working with files, plus a small daily allowance for features such as image generation and deep research.</p>
</article>
</div>
<div class="col-md-6">
<article class="panel panel-default hub-action-card">
<span class="glyphicon glyphicon-flash" aria-hidden="true"></span>
<h3>Advanced use that draws on credits</h3>
<p>More capable reasoning models, longer research and agent tasks, and coding tools such as Codex. Each use deducts credits, and heavier tasks consume more.</p>
</article>
</div>
</div>
<aside class="hub-quote-card" aria-labelledby="chatgpt-edu-credits-limit-heading">
<span class="glyphicon glyphicon-info-sign" aria-hidden="true"></span>
<h3 id="chatgpt-edu-credits-limit-heading">Shared campus credits are limited</h3>
<p>Within the one-year contract, UC San Diego has a set credit allocation purchased in advance and shared across campus, and OpenAI sets how many credits each feature uses. If campus use exceeds that allocation during the year, the university is charged for the difference. Access to credit-based features therefore has to be planned: who can use them, how much, and what happens when a group needs more than its share.</p>
<p>UC San Diego has not yet decided which credit-based features will be enabled or how credits will be shared. Until those decisions are made, descriptions from OpenAI or other UC campuses may differ from what UC San Diego accounts include.</p>
</aside>
</section>
