_Week of April 27, 2026_

## What's New in Your AI Tools

### TritonGPT

-   **[New LLMs, UCSD Assistant tools, and search improvements (April 14)](https://tritonai.ucsd.edu/tritongpt/release-notes/4-14-2026-release.html)**: TritonGPT now offers GPT 5.4, Gemini 3.1 Pro, Gemini 3.1 Flash, Claude Sonnet 4.6, and Gemma 4 26b as model choices. The older OpenAI 4 Series has been retired. The UC San Diego Assistant can now look up campus parking and check how busy locations such as dining halls and libraries are. Internet Search also returns more current results with fewer errors.

### Zoom AI Companion

-   **[Zoom Workplace 7.0.3: Hub tab added to mobile app (April 21)](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0061222)**: A new dedicated Hub tab is now visible in the Zoom Workplace mobile app (iOS and Android), giving you one place to find your recordings, docs, and other Zoom assets without digging through menus. The update also fixes a bug with sharing PDFs in meetings on iOS.

### Microsoft Copilot with Data Protection

-   **[Microsoft 365 Copilot Readiness and Resiliency with SharePoint and M365 Backup (April 22)](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoft365copilotblog/microsoft-365-copilot-readiness-and-resiliency-with-sharepoint-and-m365-backupar/4513048)**: Microsoft published guidance on new content governance features tied to SharePoint and M365 Backup. It covers data protection policies for IT and compliance teams managing Copilot deployments.

### Copilot for Microsoft 365

-   **[Anthropic Claude Opus 4.7 now available in Microsoft 365 Copilot (April 16)](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoft365copilotblog/available-today-anthropic-claude-opus-4-7-in-microsoft-365-copilot/4511666)**: Claude Opus 4.7 is available in Copilot Chat and Copilot Studio, with a rollout in Copilot for Excel. It is designed to follow instructions and check its output during complex, multi-step tasks. You can select it from the model picker.

### Google Gemini & NotebookLM

-   **[April Gemini Drop: Notebooks integration, Mac app, and more (April 25)](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/gemini-drop-april-2026/)**: The April Gemini update adds NotebookLM's research and organization features to a new Notebooks section in Gemini. The update also includes a native Mac app and personalized images that can use connected Google account context.

### TritonAI Developer API

-   **New model added this week**: \`gpt-5.5\` appeared in the TritonAI Developer API model hub since the April 23 snapshot. It supports vision, web search, and reasoning, with a 1,050,000-token context window. It is the first model in the hub to surface native web search.

## TritonAI News

-   **[New conversational AI tool helps people decide when to seek medical care (April 21)](https://today.ucsd.edu/story/new-conversational-ai-tool-uses-trusted-medical-protocols-to-help-people-decide-when-to-seek-care)**: UC San Diego researchers built an AI tool grounded in clinical protocols. It asks symptom questions and recommends whether and when to seek care. The goal is to reduce unnecessary ER visits and delayed care.
-   **[AI-enhanced microscopy produces real-time video inside live cells (April 18)](https://today.ucsd.edu/story/ai-enhanced-microscopy-produces-crisp-real-time-video-inside-live-cells)**: A UC San Diego team combined AI with advanced microscopy to capture high-resolution video inside living cells in real time. The team says the method could speed research in drug development and biology.
