## What's New in Your AI Tools

### TritonGPT

-   **[AI Contract Reviewer and Transcript Matcher now in production](https://tritonai.ucsd.edu/tritongpt/release-notes/index.html)** — Two new human-supervised workflows are live. Contract Review compares contract language with approved UC San Diego positions and prepares redlines for a reviewer. Transcript Matching compares incoming transcript documents with student records and routes uncertain cases to staff, with 99.86% accuracy across 3,700+ records in its validation sample.

### Zoom AI Companion

-   **[Zoom retires the "AI Companion" name and launches a refreshed Workplace interface](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0061222)** — Zoom has dropped the "AI Companion" brand; the features are now labeled simply "AI" throughout Zoom Workplace. Starting August 3, a redesigned interface rolls out with a new AI panel that lets you ask questions about past meetings and during live meetings. Late joiners get an automatic AI catch-up prompt summarizing what they missed.

### Microsoft Copilot with Data Protection

-   **[New Domain Exclusion control for admins](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoft365copilotblog/more-control-over-web-grounding-with-domain-exclusion-for-microsoft-365-copilot/4540151)** — Administrators can now specify up to 1,000 external domains to exclude from Copilot's web grounding, giving organizations more precise control over which sources influence AI-generated responses. Configured via PowerShell; available now.

### Copilot for Microsoft 365

-   **[July 2026 roundup: new models, Word workflows, and Excel brand kit](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoft365copilotblog/what%E2%80%99s-new-in-microsoft-365-copilot--july-2026/4538332)** — GPT-5.6 and Claude Sonnet 5 are now available as model choices in Copilot across Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and Cowork. Word gains Track Changes support for Copilot edits plus automatic table-of-contents management. Excel gets a brand-kit skill for applying approved organizational colors and fonts to charts and tables.

### Google Gemini & NotebookLM

-   **[NotebookLM is now Gemini Notebook with a new address and code execution](https://www.nlmtools.com/blog/notebooklm-is-now-gemini-notebook)** — Google renamed NotebookLM to Gemini Notebook on July 16. As of August 1, the app's address moved from notebooklm.google.com to notebook.google.com (old links redirect automatically). The update also adds a "secure cloud computer" to each notebook, letting you run code for data analysis directly on your sources. Existing notebooks are unaffected.
