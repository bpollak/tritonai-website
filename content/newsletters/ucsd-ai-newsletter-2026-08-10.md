_Monday, August 10, 2026_

## What's New in Your AI Tools

### TritonGPT

-   **[AI Contract Reviewer and Transcript Matcher now in production](https://tritonai.ucsd.edu/tritongpt/release-notes/index.html)** — Two human-supervised workflows are live. Contract Review compares language against approved positions and prepares redlines for a reviewer. Transcript Matching compares incoming transcripts with student records and routes uncertain cases to staff, with 99.86% accuracy across 3,700+ records in its published validation sample.

### Zoom AI Companion

-   **[AI Companion adds persistent memory and expanded workflow automation](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0061222)** — AI Companion can now remember your work preferences and communication style across sessions, producing more personalized responses over time. Zoom also expanded its no-code workflow automation tools for recurring tasks.

### Microsoft Copilot with Data Protection

-   **[Domain Exclusion for Copilot web grounding has been rolled back](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoft365copilotblog/update-domain-exclusion-for-microsoft-365-copilot/4543648)** — Microsoft pulled back the feature that let admins exclude specific domains from Copilot web search results. The capability is being reevaluated. Admins who configured domain exclusions should expect an update from Microsoft before the feature returns.

### Copilot for Microsoft 365

-   **[Meeting recaps app comes to Teams](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoft365copilotblog/what%E2%80%99s-new-in-microsoft-365-copilot--july-2026/4538332)** — A new Meeting recaps app pins AI-generated meeting summaries in the Teams sidebar. You can filter by meeting, browse 30 days of recaps, and generate audio summaries to catch up on the go. Also new this month: Copilot in Excel can ground analysis in Power BI data, Copilot in Word supports Track Changes review workflows, and Copilot Notebooks now accept Markdown and TXT files as references.

### TritonAI Developer API

-   **[13 new models added to the public Model Hub](https://tritonai-api.ucsd.edu/ui/model_hub_table/)** — This week's catalog expansion adds Claude Opus 4.7, 4.8, and 5; Claude Sonnet 4.6; Gemini 3.5 Flash and 3.5 Flash Lite; Gemini 3.6 Flash; Kimi K2.5 and K2.6; MiniMax M2; DeepSeek V4 Flash; Gemma 4 31B; and a Bedrock-hosted Claude Opus 4.6 variant. Developers now have 28 public models to choose from, up from 15 last week.
