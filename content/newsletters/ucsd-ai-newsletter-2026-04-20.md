_Week of April 20, 2026. Here is what changed in UC San Diego's supported AI services._

## What's New in Your AI Tools

### TritonGPT

-   **[New LLMs, UCSD Assistant tools, and search improvements](https://tritonai.ucsd.edu/tritongpt/release-notes/4-14-2026-release.html)** — TritonGPT added GPT 5.4, Gemini 3.1 Pro and Flash, Claude Sonnet 4.6, and Gemma 4 26b. The UC San Diego Assistant gained Parking and Busyness tools, while Internet Search now returns more reliable results.

### Zoom AI Companion

-   **[When action catches up to ideas: the next phase of work](https://www.zoom.com/en/blog/the-next-phase-of-work/)** — Zoom says AI Companion can draft next steps, pull context from past conversations, and update connected systems. Staff can spend less time cleaning up after a meeting and still carry the action items forward.

### Microsoft Copilot with Data Protection

-   **[Microsoft 365 Copilot readiness and resiliency with SharePoint and M365 Backup/Archive](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoft365copilotblog/microsoft-365-copilot-readiness-and-resiliency-with-sharepoint-and-m365-backupar/4513048)** — Microsoft added controls for oversharing and site access reviews. It also added restricted content discovery and backup planning around Copilot-connected material. Those controls reduce surprises when Copilot pulls from shared files.

### Copilot for Microsoft 365

-   **[Available today: Anthropic Claude Opus 4.7 in Microsoft 365 Copilot](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoft365copilotblog/available-today-anthropic-claude-opus-4-7-in-microsoft-365-copilot/4511666)** — Microsoft added Claude Opus 4.7 to parts of Microsoft 365 Copilot, with a rollout in Copilot for Excel. Staff can choose it for multi-step work that needs more careful analysis.

### Google Gemini & NotebookLM

-   **[The Gemini app is now on Mac](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/gemini-app-now-on-mac-os/)** — Google released a native Gemini app for macOS with an Option + Space shortcut and window sharing. Staff on a Mac can open it quickly for summaries, drafting help, or spreadsheet work.

## TritonAI News

-   **[AI-enhanced Microscopy Produces Crisp, Real-time Video Inside Live Cells](https://today.ucsd.edu/story/ai-enhanced-microscopy-produces-crisp-real-time-video-inside-live-cells)** — UC San Diego researchers combined microscopy with AI to produce clearer live-cell video in real time.
