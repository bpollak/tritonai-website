_Week of April 13, 2026. Here is what changed in UC San Diego's supported AI services._

## What's New in Your AI Tools

### Zoom AI Companion

-   **[When action catches up to ideas: the next phase of work](https://www.zoom.com/en/blog/the-next-phase-of-work/)** — AI Companion can now draft next steps, pull context from past conversations, and tee up actions after a meeting. That can reduce follow-up work and dropped action items.

### Microsoft Copilot with Data Protection

-   **[Latest enhancements for Copilot security, management, and analytics](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoft365copilotblog/latest-enhancements-for-copilot-security-management-and-analytics/4508476)** — Microsoft added controls for sensitive prompts, web search, and oversharing. New adoption reports give campus IT more information about how protected Copilot is being used.

### Copilot for Microsoft 365

-   **[Copilot in Word: New Capabilities for Document Workflows](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoft365copilotblog/copilot-in-word-new-capabilities-for-document-workflows/4508974)** — Copilot in Word can handle tracked changes, comments, and tables of contents. It can also update headers and footers inside the document. Those controls matter for policy drafts and other shared files with a review history.

### Google Gemini & NotebookLM

-   **[Try notebooks in Gemini to easily keep track of projects](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/notebooks-gemini-notebooklm/)** — Google introduced notebooks in Gemini that sync with NotebookLM. Chats, files, and project context stay together across both tools. Staff can return to a longer project with its source material still organized.
