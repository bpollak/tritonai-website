## What's New in Your AI Tools

### TritonGPT

-   **[UC San Diego Assistant launches in the UC San Diego mobile app](https://tritonai.ucsd.edu/tritongpt/release-notes/index.html)** — The TritonGPT-powered UC San Diego Assistant is now available directly in the campus mobile app. Ask questions about campus resources, policies, and services on the go without opening a separate browser tab. Great for quick answers between meetings or while walking across campus.

### Zoom AI Companion

-   **["AI Companion" is now just "AI" — and it's faster on mobile](https://releasebot.io/updates/zoom/zoom-workplace)** — Zoom's June 2026 update retires the "AI Companion" brand. Meeting Summaries, Chat Compose, Scheduling Assistance, and Smart Recording remain available under the new name. Zoom Slides, Sheets, and Paper are also available from the mobile app.

### Microsoft Copilot with Data Protection

-   **[Copilot Notebooks roll out to more users across your team](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoft365copilotblog/what%E2%80%99s-new-in-notebooks--june-2026/4525625)** — Copilot Notebooks is a shared workspace for project files, document questions, and mind maps. It is now rolling out to Copilot Chat users as well as Microsoft 365 Copilot subscribers. Teams with mixed licenses can work in the same notebook through the Microsoft 365 Copilot app or OneNote on the web.

### Copilot for Microsoft 365

-   **[Learning Agent is now generally available](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoft365copilotblog/learning-agent-now-generally-available-%E2%80%94-personalized-ai-upskilling-for-every-em/4524571)** — Microsoft's Learning Agent recommends training based on your role and work patterns. It appears inside Copilot and Microsoft 365 apps. Each day starts with an AI Tip of the Day tied to the work you do.

### Google Gemini & NotebookLM

-   **[NotebookLM gets its biggest update yet — powered by Gemini 3.5](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/08/notebooklms-new-update-will-help-you-build-source-repository-from-chat/)** — NotebookLM can build a source library from a conversation. It suggests relevant sources and searches for material tied to the project. Exports now include documents, spreadsheets, presentations, and structured data. The update is available to Google AI Ultra users and Workspace business customers with AI access.
