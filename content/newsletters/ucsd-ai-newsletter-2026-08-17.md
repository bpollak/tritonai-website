## What's New in Your AI Tools

### Zoom AI Companion

-   **[Zoom Voice Translator reaches general availability](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0061222)** — Zoom's real-time spoken-language translator is now generally available in meetings, letting participants who speak different languages communicate without manual interpretation. It supports English, Chinese, French, Japanese, and Spanish, and integrates with existing AI-powered translated captions. Also in this release: My Notes gains quick-capture on iOS via Home Screen widget and Control Center, and Zoom Phone calls can now activate real-time transcription with speaker-attributed captions that save automatically to the My Notes hub.

### Microsoft Copilot with Data Protection

-   **[Domain Exclusion gives admins more control over Copilot web grounding](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoft365copilotblog/update-domain-exclusion-for-microsoft-365-copilot/4543648)** — Admins can now exclude specific domains from Copilot's web-grounded responses, so certain sites will not be used as source material when Copilot pulls information from the web. This is useful when you want to block competitor sites, unreliable sources, or internal domains from influencing AI-generated answers. The feature was updated in early August with refinements based on early feedback.

### Copilot for Microsoft 365

-   **[Copilot Notebooks now works with Markdown, plain-text, and rich-text files](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoft365copilotblog/copilot-notebooks-now-works-with-markdown-plain-text-and-rich-text-files/4545652)** — You can now bring READMEs, wikis, logs, transcripts, and rich-text files into Copilot Notebooks alongside the documents you already use. This means more of your existing project material can serve as context for Copilot without reformatting. If your team keeps notes in Markdown or exports transcripts as plain text, those files now work directly in the notebook experience.

### Google Gemini & NotebookLM

-   **[New connected apps are coming to Gemini](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/new-connected-apps-services-gemini-august-2026/)** — Gemini is rolling out the ability to connect external services so you can plan, create, and manage tasks in one place. New integrations include productivity tools like Granola and Otter.ai for meeting summaries, Wix for website editing, OpenTable and Ticketmaster for bookings and events, iHeartRadio and Pandora for music, and Angi, Thumbtack, and Zocdoc for home and health services. Connecting is optional and can be turned on per service in Gemini settings. Also this week, Google announced [Gemini 3.7 Flash](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-3-7-flash/), a new model with stronger coding and document-reasoning capabilities now available to Pro and Ultra subscribers in Gemini chat.
