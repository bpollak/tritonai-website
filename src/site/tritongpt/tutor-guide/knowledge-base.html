Update the TritonAI Tutor's Knowledge Base

The knowledge base is the set of materials your tutor can retrieve from when it answers a student.

What the Tutor Can Index

From Canvas

  • Pages
  • Assignments
  • Announcements
  • Files
  • Modules
  • Quizzes
  • Discussions
  • Syllabus

Outside Canvas

  • Google Drive folders
  • Websites

Updating which Canvas materials the tutor ingests is done exclusively from the tutor configuration page. If you want the tutor to use Google Drive folders and websites, you'll need to add those first in the Knowledge tab.

For how hidden files, lock dates, and quiz content are handled, see the Tutor FAQ.

Add or Remove Canvas Material

Adding or removing Canvas material from your tutor's knowledge base is done in the tutor configuration tab.

  1. Within your Canvas course, click TritonAI Tutor in the course navigation on the left side of the screen.
  2. Click the Tutors tab if you don't land on it by default.
  3. Click the pencil icon next to the tutor you want to edit.
  4. Scroll down to the “Knowledge” section.
  5. Select the Canvas tab on the left side of the section if it's not already selected.
  6. Check any boxes for material you want to be indexed. Un-check any boxes you do not want indexed.

    You can click the chevron (>) next to any folder to go into subfolders and choose individual materials.

  7. When you've made your selections, scroll to the top of the page and click Save.

Add Material in Google Drive

Adding the material from a Google Drive folder to your tutor's knowledge base is a little different than adding Canvas materials. Here's how to do it.

  1. Starting in your Canvas course, click the TritonGPT AI Tutor link in your course navigation.
  2. Click on the Knowledge icon in the tutor navigation near the top of the page. It looks like a little book.
  3. Scroll down to the bottom of the page and you'll see a section called Google Drive. In the text field, you'll want to paste the link to that Google Drive folder.
  4. Go to that Google Drive folder in your browser. To get a link to it, click the folder name near the top of the page, select Share from the menu, and then click Copy link.
  5. Switch back to your Canvas tab. Paste that URL into the field in the Google Drive section and click Add Folder.
  6. The first time you do this, you may be prompted to authorize a connection between Onyx and your Google Drive. Approve all the steps needed.
  7. Once you do, you should see that the Google Drive folder is scheduled to sync. After a little while, that status will change to Indexed, which indicates that you can now configure your tutor to ingest whatever materials you like.
  8. In the tutor navigation near the top of the page, click the Tutors icon. Then click the pencil icon next to the tutor you want to edit. Scroll down to the Knowledge section and click Google Drive. You should now see the folder you added. Just like with any other materials, you can use the chevron next to the folder name to drill into it and select only what you want this tutor to ingest. When you've selected all the folders you want, click Save near the top of the page.

Add Websites

The TritonAI Tutor can also index websites to use as part of its knowledge base. Like with Google Drive folders, you add the site in the knowledge tab and then tell the specific tutor which websites you want it to ingest.

  1. Begin in the Canvas course with your tutor, and click TritonGPT AI Tutor in the course navigation.
  2. Click the knowledge icon in the vertical navigation bar near the top of the page.
  3. Scroll down to the Websites section. Paste the URL of the site you want to index and click Add Website.
  4. Once the site's status shows as indexed, open the tutor configuration and select the website from the Knowledge section. When you've selected all the sites you want, click Save.
