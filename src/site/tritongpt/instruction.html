Instructional AI

Attention Instructors!

TritonAI is now natively integrated with Canvas, UC San Diego's campuswide learning management system! You are invited to have your Canvas course(s) connected, and your enrolled students can use a TritonGPT-powered AI Tutor to interact with course content.

Key features include:

  • You pre-select the approved Canvas content areas and websites the tutor may retrieve from.
  • Exclude any material you do not want students to know about in advance (for example, assignments or study guides).
  • Acts as a Socratic tutor: Guides students toward answers and asks them to explain their reasoning.
  • All AI models available in the Triton AI tutor meet strict UC San Diego and UC data security and privacy standards, including your data not being used to inform other users' responses.

How It Works for Students

Each participating course can receive a student-facing AI Tutor configured for that course and made available through the approved course access path.

The AI Tutor is designed to help students engage more deeply with course materials:

  • Acts as a Socratic tutor: Guides students toward answers and asks them to explain their reasoning.
  • Grounded in course content: Retrieves from instructor-selected material while generating a response; students should still check the cited source.
  • Self-paced 24/7 support: Tutor is also available, at your students' pace and on their study schedule.

Instructor Setup & Control

Instructors are the gatekeepers of their course AI Tutor. You control what the tutor knows and how it behaves:

  • Select course materials: Choose the approved documents and websites the tutor may retrieve from. The tutor will learn and unlearn content as you select or deselect content throughout the term.
  • Set the pedagogical style: Choose Socratic mode to guide students toward answers, or directive mode to provide direct explanations.
  • Multiple tutors per course: Create separate tutors for different units, lab sections, or study purposes.
