Instructional AI

UC San Diego's Instructional AI program provides participating instructors with secure, course-specific AI assistants powered by TritonGPT, the university's on-premise AI platform. The program is expanding for Summer Session 2 and Fall 2026.

Join the Summer Session 2 Cohort

We are actively recruiting instructors for Summer Session 2. If you are teaching this summer and want to provide your students with an AI study companion, sign up now.

Sign Up for Summer Session 2

TritonGPT uses Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to ground the AI's knowledge in course materials provided by instructors: lecture slides, study guides, readings, and recorded lecture transcripts. While the AI language model maintains general knowledge, its primary function is to act as an expert on the specific content taught in your course.

The platform supports both on-premise and cloud frontier models routed through LiteLLM, giving instructors model flexibility without compromising data governance.

How It Works for Students

Each participating course gets a custom AI Tutor — a student-facing assistant deployed through Onyx (TritonGPT's platform) and integrated directly into Canvas. Students access it from the Canvas course navigation without a separate login.

The AI Tutor is designed to help students engage more deeply with course materials:

  • Acts as a Socratic tutor: Guides students toward answers and encourages critical thinking rather than simply providing solutions.
  • Grounded in your course content: Answers student questions based strictly on the materials you provide — lecture slides, readings, study guides, recorded transcripts.
  • Configurable pedagogical style: Instructors choose between Socratic (guides toward answers) or directive (provides answers) modes.
  • Available 24/7: Students can explore topics at their own pace in a safe, interactive space.

Instructor Setup & Control

Instructors are the gatekeepers of their course AI Tutor. You control what the tutor knows and how it behaves:

  • Select course materials: Choose which Canvas documents, Google Drive files, and websites the tutor can reference. Content syncs automatically every 30 minutes.
  • Set the pedagogical style: Choose Socratic mode to guide students toward answers, or directive mode to provide direct explanations.
  • Multiple tutors per course: Create separate tutors for different units, lab sections, or study purposes.
  • Usage insights: Monthly, anonymized reports of student prompts help you understand where students are struggling or most engaged.
  • No student-inserted content: Students cannot add their own materials to the tutor's knowledge base — only instructor-approved content is used.

Getting Started: A Simple & Secure Process

The system is designed for ease of use through Canvas LTI and secure Google Drive integration.

  1. Request participation: Email AI-Pilots@ucsd.edu to join the program. Our team will help you get set up.
  2. Connect your course: Link your Canvas course, create a Google Drive folder with your materials, and/or add website URLs for additional reading. The AI Tutor will automatically sync new or updated content every 30 minutes.
  3. Configure your tutor: Select which documents the tutor can reference and choose your preferred pedagogical style (Socratic or directive).
  4. Students access via Canvas: The AI Tutor appears in your Canvas course navigation. Students can start using it immediately — no separate login required.

Supported File Types

The platform supports Google Docs, Sheets, Slides, Microsoft Office files (Word, Excel, PowerPoint), PDF, TXT, CSV, MD, HTML, and JSON.

AI Models

Queries are routed through UC San Diego's model gateway, which provides access to UC-hosted open models and approved cloud frontier models. Model selection is configured for the instructional use case.

Support & Logistics

  • Collaboration: TAs and co-instructors can be added to help manage the course AI Tutor.
  • Canvas Integration: The AI Tutor appears in the Canvas course navigation for enrolled students.
  • Usage Insights: Monthly, anonymized reports of student prompts help instructors understand where students are struggling or most engaged.
  • Dedicated Support: The program team provides full technical support to participants throughout the quarter.
  • Onboarding: New instructors receive guided setup and documentation before the term begins.
  • Security: All interactions stay within UC San Diego's secure infrastructure. No data is shared with third-party training pipelines.
