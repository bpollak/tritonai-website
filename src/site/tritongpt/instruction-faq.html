Instructional AI: Frequently Asked Questions

Answers for instructors and students using the TritonAI Tutor in Canvas.

The tutor is connected to your Canvas course and retrieves from content the instructor selects. Setting up a tutor? See the Tutor Guide for step-by-step instructions on the knowledge base and system prompt.

For Instructors

What is the Instructional AI program?

TritonAI is integrated with Canvas. A participating course gets a student-facing TritonAI Tutor that answers questions using course materials you approve. You decide what it can read and how it responds.

How do I add a TritonAI tutor to my Canvas course?

Email the team at AI-Pilots@ucsd.edu with the subject line “TritonAI tutor.” The team verifies your request, configures the tutor for your course, and sends you a Getting Started guide and video.

Email AI-Pilots@ucsd.edu

Is our course data used to train AI models?

Models available in the tutor meet UC San Diego and UC data security and privacy standards, and your data is not used to inform other users’ responses. See the Privacy Statement and Trust, Privacy & Hosting for details.

Can I have more than one tutor in a course?

Yes. You can create separate tutors for different units, lab sections, or study purposes, each with its own content and teaching style.

What is the difference between Socratic and directive mode?

Socratic mode guides students toward answers with questions and asks them to explain their reasoning. Directive mode gives direct explanations. Choose per tutor based on what the tutor is for; for example, Socratic for problem-set practice and directive for reviewing course logistics or definitions.

Which parts of my Canvas course does the tutor index?

The Canvas connector can index:

  • Pages, Assignments, Announcements, Discussions, and the Syllabus
  • Files and Modules
  • Quizzes (Classic and New Quizzes)

It fetches content by type, so material can be indexed even if it is not placed in a Module. You choose which indexed sources the tutor may retrieve from in its knowledge settings. Changing a Canvas item’s availability does not immediately remove an indexed copy; see the refresh guidance below.

Can the tutor read my quizzes?

Yes, if quizzes are selected. Classic Quizzes include question and answer text. New Quizzes include prompts and answer-choice text.

Quiz content is indexed so the tutor can be instructed to recognize quiz questions and decline to answer them. If you would rather the tutor never see quiz content, leave quizzes unselected in the tutor’s knowledge settings.

Can I add sources outside Canvas?

Yes. The tutor editor supports Google Drive folders and web pages as additional sources. These are attached separately from the Canvas connector.

Does the tutor respect module and item lock dates?

The connector checks item and parent Module availability when indexing content. It does not check lock status during each conversation. Material indexed before you re-lock it can remain searchable until cleanup; deselect it in the tutor’s knowledge settings or contact the TritonAI team to remove it promptly.

Due dates do not hide content. Keep solutions and exam keys unpublished until release, and review the selected sources before students use the tutor.

How quickly do content changes reach the tutor?

The connector refreshes every 30 minutes and removes content that is no longer available every 30 days. Two edge cases follow from this:

  • Re-locking something already indexed. The tutor does not check Canvas lock status live. If an item was indexed and you later lock it, it can remain searchable until the next 30-day cleanup. To remove it right away, deselect it in the tutor’s knowledge settings or contact the TritonAI team.
  • Date-only unlocks. Indexing picks up items based on when they were last updated. An item that unlocks on a date without being edited may not appear in the tutor as soon as it unlocks.

Tip: Keep sensitive material (solutions, exam keys) unpublished until release, rather than publishing it early behind a lock date. That avoids both edge cases.

Where do I get help or report a problem?

Email AI-Pilots@ucsd.edu for setup questions, content changes, or to report a problem with tutor responses. General TritonAI feedback goes to tritonai@ucsd.edu.

For Students

What is the AI Tutor?

An AI study assistant set up by your instructor for your course. It answers questions using course materials your instructor has approved, and it is available any time you are studying.

How do I open it?

Your instructor makes the tutor available inside your Canvas course. Check the course navigation or announcements for the link. You sign in through Canvas; there is no separate account to create.

Are my prompts used to train AI models?

Models available in the tutor meet UC San Diego and UC data security and privacy standards, and your data is not used to inform other users’ responses. See the Privacy Statement and Trust, Privacy & Hosting for details.

Why does it ask me questions instead of giving the answer?

Many tutors are set to Socratic mode. Instead of handing you an answer, the tutor guides you toward it and asks you to explain your reasoning. This is intentional: working through the steps is how the material sticks. Some tutors use a directive mode that explains more directly; your instructor chooses which.

Will it help with quizzes, exams, or graded work?

Follow your instructor’s AI policy for the course. Tutors can be set to decline quiz questions. The tutor uses indexed course sources selected by your instructor; changes to Canvas availability may take time to reach that index. Ask your instructor if the tutor shows material you believe should not be available.

What does the tutor know about?

Only the course content your instructor selects, such as Pages, Modules, files, and the syllabus, plus any websites or Google Drive folders they add. It does not see your grades or submissions. If it cannot find something in the course materials, it may say so or answer less reliably.

Can my instructor see my conversations?

Your instructor can request to review all conversations for the course, but your name and email are not shown to them. Each conversation is grouped on its own, and conversations are not linked to you across sessions in what your instructor sees.

Anything you type is visible, though. Avoid putting your name, student ID, or other personal details in your messages.

Please note: Because generative AI can still be incomplete or wrong, students should verify important answers against course sources and instructor guidance.
