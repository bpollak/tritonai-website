TritonGPT uses one campus AI platform across several channels. Choose the entry point that fits where you are working and the kind of help you need.

The signed-in workspace provides model choice, document uploads, conversation history, and purpose-built campus assistants.

The UC San Diego Assistant brings the campus AI service into the official app with text, voice, and mobile-aware context.

Campus website widget

Approved public sites can add a site-grounded assistant that people use without signing in. The site owner maintains its sources and support path.

Explore the website widget

Mobile assistant

Silent demonstration showing a campus question entered in the UC San Diego Assistant and an answer using current campus information.

What the mobile channel adds

  • Campus app access: Students, faculty, and staff can reach the assistant from the official UC San Diego app on iOS and Android.
  • Text and voice: The interface supports typed questions and hands-free interaction.
  • Live campus context: Available signals can include parking, library busyness, transit, events, and location-aware information.

Website assistant widget

Silent demonstration showing the UC San Diego Assistant answering a policy question inside a campus website and citing supporting content.

What the widget provides

  • Public access: Visitors can ask questions without a campus sign-in.
  • Grounded answers: Responses use the approved public content selected for that service.
  • Lightweight integration: A standard JavaScript embed connects the site to the shared service.
  • Local ownership: Each site maintains its source content, accessibility review, support contact, and escalation path.

Read the widget guidance

Blink AI overview

Blink search can use TritonGPT to combine relevant campus policies and procedures into a concise overview. The overview gives people a starting point, outlines useful next steps, and keeps the supporting campus sources available for review.

Shared platform responsibilities

The shared platform supplies common capabilities such as model access, service operations, and campus authentication where required. Each channel still needs a named owner, reviewed source content, an accessibility and privacy review, and a clear place for questions, corrections, and escalation.
